In many rural regions, livestock owners cannot quickly identify diseases affecting cattle, goats, sheep, or poultry. Veterinary hospitals are often far away, regular health monitoring is limited, and farmers usually depend on experience rather than scientific diagnosis. Because of delayed detection, diseases spread rapidly among animals, causing financial loss, reduced milk/meat production, and sometimes death of livestock. Lack of digital tracking and awareness also makes disease outbreaks difficult to control.

Proposed Solution Overview:

The project proposes a smart AI-powered livestock healthcare platform that combines:

WEB application </br> AI disease prediction <br> Alert notification system.

AI Integration Possibilities:

Artificial Intelligence can be used to build a smart livestock monitoring system with features such as:

Image-Based Disease Detection. </br>
AI analyzes photos of animal eyes, skin, mouth, or wounds to identify possible diseases. </br>
Symptom Prediction System. </br>
Farmers enter symptoms like fever, reduced eating, coughing, or abnormal movement, and AI predicts likely diseases.
